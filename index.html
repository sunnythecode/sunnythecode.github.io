OXE-AugE: A Large-Scale Robot Augmentation of OXE for Scaling Cross-Embodiment Policy Learning
International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML), 2026 (Spotlight, 2.2%)
Guanhua Ji*, Harsha Polavaram*, Lawrence Yunliang Chen*, Sandeep Bajamahal, Zehan Ma, Simeon Adebola, Chenfeng Xu, Ken Goldberg
[website] [paper] [dataset] [video]

We study how scaling robot augmentation impacts cross-embodiment learning and introduce OXE-AugE, a large-scale augmented version of the Open X-Embodiment dataset. Augmenting demonstrations across robot embodiments improves policy generalization to unseen robots, achieving 24–45% higher success on real-world manipulation tasks.

GaP: A Graph-as-Policy Multi-Agent Self-Learning Harness For Variational Automation Tasks
In Review, 2026
K. Chen*, S. Xie*, L. Fu, J. Yu, W. Pacini, S. Bajamahal, H. Kim, J. Drake, D. Kim, H. Xue, J. Francis, C. Juette, P. Schaldenbrand, M. Y. Seker, R. Wickramarachchi, U. Yoo, G. Wang, A. Murali, B. Sundaralingam, S. S. Sastry, S. Huang, Y. Zhu, L. "Jim" Fan, K. Goldberg
[website] [paper] [code]

We introduce GaP, a multi-agent robotics harness that turns natural-language tasks into executable computation graphs. Through simulation rehearsal and feedback-driven refinement, GaP achieves up to 99% success across variational automation tasks and transfers to real-world grocery packing, popcorn making, and sim bimanual crate washing.
MonoDuo: Using One Robot Arm to Learn Bimanual Policies
Sandeep Bajamahal*, Lawrence Yunliang Chen*, Toru Lin, Zehan Ma, Jitendra Malik, Ken Goldberg
IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA), 2026
[website] [video]

We study how to learn bimanual robot policies without relying on scarce bimanual robots or datasets. Our framework, MonoDuo, uses single-arm robot demonstrations paired with human collaboration and augments them into synthetic bimanual data using pose estimation and segmentation. This enables up to 70% zero-shot success on unseen bimanual robots.
